Swing Score Steps:

1 Calculate individual player PIE average (either season long or rolling average up to that point)

2 Split games into above and below based on average 

3 Calculate the team's average point differential, then the average point differential for above PIE games and below PIE games

4 Calculate above impact and below impact by subtracting the above point diff from the season average point diff, and the below point diff form the season average point diff

5 Finally calculate swing score by subtracting the above impact from below
